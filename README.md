\# Mellish's Penthouse - Public World Images



This repository contains public images used throughout my VRChat worlds.



Most of the files here are:



\* Advertisement posters

\* Event posters

\* Information boards

\* World decoration assets

\* Screenshots taken within VRChat

\* Photos of myself, friends, and community members

\* Reference images used to add atmosphere and personality to my worlds



These images are primarily used to make my worlds feel more lived-in and personal, while also allowing Quest users to see content that would otherwise only be visible on PC. In many cases, the images show avatars, events, and memories from the community that helped shape the worlds.



This repository serves as a convenient public host for these assets and allows them to be updated independently of the worlds themselves when required.



\## About Mellish's Penthouse



Mellish's Penthouse is a collection of VRChat worlds focused on community spaces, social experiences, creative projects, and adult-oriented environments for verified adults.



Many of the posters and images found here are displayed throughout those worlds to provide information, advertise events, showcase community members, and add a personal touch to the environments.



\## Notes



\* All images are intended for use within my VRChat worlds.

\* Some images may contain avatars belonging to community members who have given permission for their use.

\* Files may be added, updated, or removed over time as worlds evolve.



If you've somehow found this repository directly, you're essentially looking at the digital photo album hanging on the walls of my VRChat worlds.
